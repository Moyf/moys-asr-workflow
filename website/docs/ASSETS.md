## 声音纸感

- 文件：`website/public/home/sound-paper.webp`，1536 × 1024，约 245 KB。
- 生成方式：内置 imagegen；原始生成结果转为 WebP，不包含 EXIF / XMP。
- 用途：首屏装饰材质，不代表产品界面、测量结果或真实声音数据。
- 提示词：

```text

Create a photographic tactile material study, warm ivory cotton paper with ultra-fine visible paper fibers, soft blue-gray ink diffusion through the fibers, restrained muted indigo and slate mineral pigment sweeping in a broad, organic horizontal sound-like contour near the lower third. Quiet, elegant, scientific yet human, like a close photograph of handmade printmaking paper under very soft raking daylight. Large calm negative space across upper two thirds; very low contrast overall so dark typographic UI remains readable. Wide landscape composition about 3:2. Matte, physically plausible, refined editorial design, no text, no letters, no logos, no interface, no objects, no glass balls, no glossy 3D, no neon, no cosmic stars, no vector grids, no repeated CSS-style lines or obvious waveform bars. Intended to support a warm-white and ink-blue website.
```

## 产品图

首页沿用 PR 236 的 `public/home/` 截图，编辑器图来自仓库软件素材。Launcher 的 `launcher-done.webp`、`step-transcribe.webp` 沿用 PR 235 静态设计原型，仍属于设计预览，不是已发布软件截图。
